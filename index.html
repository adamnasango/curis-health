Who we are

About Curis Health.

Curis Health is a leading supplier of medical equipment, human medicine, and chemicals to various industries. We are committed to delivering innovative and reliable solutions for healthcare professionals and patients, with a focus on integrity, excellence, and customer satisfaction. Curis Health strives to exceed expectations, understands the challenges faced by healthcare professionals, and aims to provide personalized service and support to ensure client success.

As a company, we believe that our success is measured by the success of our clients. That is why we work tirelessly to build lasting relationships with each and every one of our customers. We are committed to providing personalized service and support to ensure that our clients receive the products they need, when they need them.

Foundational Principles

Philosophy, Vision & Mission

Philosophy

At Curis Health, we believe that every individual deserves access to high quality healthcare products and services. We are committed to providing our clients with exceptional products and services that improve healthcare outcomes and make a positive difference in the lives of patients.

Vision

Our vision is to be a leading provider of medical equipment, chemicals, and medicines in the healthcare industry. We strive to be a trusted partner to our clients, providing exceptional products and services that meet their unique needs and exceed their expectations.

Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality medical equipment, chemicals, and medicines to healthcare facilities across the country. We are committed to sourcing the best products from reputable manufacturers and providing exceptional customer service to our clients.

Key Pillars

Our Core Values

  • Quality

    We are committed to providing our clients with products and services of the highest quality, ensuring that they meet the strictest standards of safety and effectiveness.

  • Integrity

    We conduct our business with the highest levels of honesty, transparency, and ethical behavior. We value the trust that our clients place in us and strive to maintain that trust in everything we do.

  • Innovation

    We are constantly seeking new and better ways to meet the needs of our clients and improve healthcare outcomes. We encourage creativity and innovation in our team members and are always looking for ways to improve our products and services.

  • Collaboration

    We believe that working together is essential to achieving our goals. We foster a collaborative and inclusive work environment that encourages teamwork, open communication, and mutual respect.

  • Customer Service

    We are dedicated to providing exceptional customer service to our clients. We prioritize their needs and are always ready to provide expert advice and support to meet their unique requirements.

Organizational Framework

Our Company Structure

Curis Health has a board of directors, an executive team, sales and marketing, procurement and supply chain, operations, quality assurance, finance and accounting, and human resources. We work together to provide high-quality healthcare products and services while upholding ethical standards.

The board of directors provides strategic direction and oversight for the company. It is responsible for setting the company's vision, mission, and goals, as well as ensuring that the company operates in compliance with legal and ethical standards.
