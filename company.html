The board of directors provides strategic direction and oversight for the company. It is responsible for setting the company's vision, mission, and goals, as well as ensuring that the company operates in compliance with legal and ethical standards.

Consists of Managing Director and other top level heads of Departments /Managers.

The sales and marketing team is responsible for identifying potential clients, promoting the company's products and services, and generating revenue for the company.

The procurement and supply chain team is responsible for sourcing high-quality medical equipment, chemicals, and medicines from reputable manufacturers, negotiating contracts, and ensuring that products are delivered to clients on time.

The operations team is responsible for managing the day-to-day activities of the company, including customer service, inventory management, and logistics.

The quality assurance team is responsible for ensuring that all products and services meet the company's strict quality standards and comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

The finance and accounting team is responsible for managing the company's financial operations, including accounting, budgeting, and financial reporting.

The human resources team is responsible for managing the company's workforce, including recruitment, training, payroll, and employee relations.
